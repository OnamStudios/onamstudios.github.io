Privacy Policy

Neon City: Escape · Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: Neon City: Escape has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads & Privacy.

Who we are

Neon City: Escape (“the game”) is made by ONAM STUDIOS, an independent developer based in the Netherlands (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the game itself. Google is responsible for the data its advertising service processes.

Data stored only on your device

To save your game, the following is stored locally on your phone or tablet and is never sent to us:

  • Player profiles: the profile name you type, your chosen robot, unlocked levels and robots, scores, stars, records, credits and upgrades.
  • Settings: sound and music volume, vibration on/off.
  • A small counter used to space out ads between levels.

Please don't use your real name or other personal information as a profile name. You can delete a profile in the game at any time, and uninstalling the game removes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can watch to continue a run, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. To serve, limit, measure and protect these ads against fraud, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version).
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics.

Google may use this data to show personalized ads only where you have allowed it. Otherwise, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. You can read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, a consent message from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks whether ads may use your data. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ads & Privacy in the game.
  • Everywhere: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Legal basis (GDPR)

Saving your progress on your device is necessary to provide the game. Personalized advertising is based on your consent. Showing non-personalized ads and preventing ad fraud is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free.

Your rights

If you live in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Since we don’t keep any of your data ourselves, the quickest way to exercise these rights for ad data is through your device’s ad settings or with Google directly. You can always contact us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads.
  • Vibration: short vibrations during play. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google’s ads SDK as described above.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete a profile or uninstall the game. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
